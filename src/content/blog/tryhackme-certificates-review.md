I initially wanted to study cyber and one of the routes that I took is `TryHackMe`.
Each path contains multiple chapters, each with a different subject and the rooms contain the study material while some include VM machines.

To study and gain the most of `TryHackMe` I bought the premium subscription.

I have completed 5 paths:
- Pre Security 
- Cyber Security 101
- Web Fundamentals
- Web Application Red Teaming
- Red Teaming

For my experience with `TryHackMe` , the theory for a lot of rooms is a good foundation to certain topics but if I wanted to study something more in depth I had to do my own research which is expected, for example I while learning NTFS I found a autopsy room but bit-level manual instructions where not present.

The virtual machines were mostly unpleasant, especially Windows machines which do need a good enough hardware to run on, they were painfully slow and buggy to the point of it just wasting time trying to wait or restart them, some just crashed or had to be reconnected. 

Overall, `TryHackMe` was useful for building a theoretical foundation and getting familiar with a wide range of topics, but for deeper understanding and practical experience, I often had to supplement it with my own research and hands-on work.
